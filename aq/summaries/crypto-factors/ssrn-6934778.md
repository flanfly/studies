*Category: single-strategy.* One integrated pipeline — fundamentals screen, ML probability rank, CRRA allocation — with a model horse race (logistic vs. MLP vs. LightGBM) and a separate EVT/GPD leverage overlay inside it. Despite sitting in a `crypto-factors` directory, the paper trades US large-cap equities; crypto exposure enters only as Coinbase (COIN), a "crypto-beta" equity.

## Claim

The paper builds a six-stage "quantamental" pipeline over the S&P 500 ∪ Nasdaq-100 universe (516 tickers): (1) universe, (2) data extraction, (3) a metrics sleeve of fundamental ratios winsorized at the 1st/99th percentiles and z-scored, (4) a composite screening score, (5) machine-learning refinement producing a directional probability P̂(d_t | x_t) for up/down moves, and (6) CRRA utility-based allocation (§3). The headline screening signal is Score = EP_z + FCP_z + GPA_z − AssetGrowth_z − Accruals_z + ShYield_z (eq. 6), rewarding earnings yield, free-cash-flow yield, gross profitability, and shareholder yield while penalizing asset growth and accruals. Over 48 rolling out-of-sample monthly windows (Nov-2020 → Oct-2025), a calibrated logistic classifier (K=5 CV, Platt scaling) achieves mean AUC 0.6197, long-bucket hit rate 73.5%, short-bucket hit rate 48.1% (Table 6). The final long-only allocation at γ = 3 concentrates in MCK (33.8%), AVGO (48.5%), PWR (17.7%) with model-implied expected return 42.4%, volatility 21.5%, Sharpe 2.15 (Table 15, §10.1). All "expected returns" are in-sample µ-vector outputs of the optimizer, not realized backtests; the only realized validation is anecdotal — AMD +23%, LLY +28%, COIN −29% over roughly one month after the Oct 15–25, 2025 run (§11).

## Fit with our constraints

Instrument classes are US large-cap equities and two ETFs (SPY, HYG); no futures, options (explicitly disallowed by the assignment, §10.3), or perpetuals appear. Shorting is expressed as negative weights in single-name equities — the short sleeve shorts COIN with w_COIN ∈ [−0.20, 0], i.e., short stock, so it does not directly translate to futures/perps as written. Long and short legs are reported separately (long vs. short hit rates, Tables 6, 8, 9). Leverage appears only in the optional "Leveraged Portfolio" section via 10:1 equity total-return swaps (TRS) on AMD and LLY financed by a COIN short (§10.3). Replication would need: 10 years of daily Yahoo Finance prices resampled to month-end, quarterly income/cash-flow/balance-sheet statements, shares-outstanding series, and Dolthub accruals (with an in-script "Sloan-light" fallback). The assignment imposes a minimum market cap of $400M (funds $400M AUM), so microcaps are excluded by construction; no explicit penny-stock or liquidity screen is described.

## Strategies

The paper is one pipeline, so this is one strategy with documented internal variants. No transaction costs, turnover, or execution lags are reported anywhere; fundamentals are TTM sums of the last four quarterly reports, and no point-in-time filing-date adjustment is described.

### Composite Z-Score screen (§3.1, §5)

Raw ratios — EP = TTM Net Income / mcap, FCP = (TTM CFO − |TTM CapExt|)/mcap, ROA, ROE, GPA = TTM(Revenue − COGS)/Total Assets, Shareholder Yield = (|TTM Dividends Paid| + |TTM Buybacks|)/mcap, AssetGrowth = (A_t − A_{t−4})/A_{t−4}, and accruals (Dolthub measure overriding the estimated balance-sheet-delta measure whenever available) — are clipped at the 1st/99th percentiles and standardized z_i = (x_i − x̄)/σ_x cross-sectionally. The composite Score (eq. 6, above) rank-orders the universe; sorting it is the screener. The authors frame it as risk-free-of-behavioural-tag fundamental quality; the counterparties are unnamed. Top decile firms cluster in software, communications, and consumer staples; bottom names are high-growth/capital-intensive (semis, industrials), and the authors note NVDA/AAPL/MSFT score poorly on EP_z and FCP_z despite strong GPA_z (§5.2.1). Appendix A documents a sector/size-neutralized variant: size residualization x̂ = α + β·log(mcap), sector-neutral z_{i,s} = (x_i − μ_s)/σ_s, tanh cap z̄ = tanh(z/2)·3 or hard cap |z| ≤ 2.5, and reweighted composite Score = 0.40·Value + 0.40·Quality + 0.20·Stability (eqs. 23–32), which was introduced after energy names dominated the raw screen during the 2022–2023 oil boom (raw scores reached 8–10σ; Table 27).

### Probabilistic Rank: logistic, MLP, LightGBM (§4, §6)

The target is next-period direction d_t ∈ {up, down} with label horizon H ∈ {12, 24, 36} months; models are trained cross-sectionally per month with a rolling, expanding out-of-sample protocol (one-step-ahead prediction for month t+1, calibration only on validation folds inside the train window). The default model is logistic regression augmented with K=5 within-window cross-validation and Platt calibration (Isotonic as fallback); variants are a two-layer MLP — Linear(d, 64) → ReLU → Dropout(0.1) → Linear(64, 1) — and LightGBM. At a single 12-month snapshot (2022-10-31), logistic AUC 0.599 vs. LightGBM AUC 0.844, LogLoss 0.6733 vs. 0.5289; logistic coefficients β_GPA_z = +0.319, β_Accruals_z = +0.112, β_0 = +0.230 (Table 2). Rolling OOS means (Table 6): logistic (K=5, calibrated) AUC 0.6197 / LogLoss 0.5289 / long hit 73.5% / short hit 48.1%; MLP 0.6122 / 0.538 / 69.7 / 54.2; LightGBM 0.6008 / 0.544 / 70.3 / 52.4; uncalibrated logistic baseline 0.6187 / 0.6733 / 72.5 / 51.6. The authors' conclusion: calibrated logistic for long-only sleeves, MLP for short selection, LightGBM as robust baseline (§6.2, §7).

### CRRA allocation (§8, §10)

Optimization: min_w (γ/2) wᵀΣw − μᵀw s.t. 1ᵀw = 1, w ≥ 0, with γ ∈ {2, 3, 4, 6}, μ and Σ estimated from 5-year monthly returns, and a recommended refinement μ̃_i = μ_hist + α_1(P̂_i − 0.5) + α_2·Score_i (eq. 17) and covariance shrinkage Σ̃ = (1−λ)Σ_hist + λΣ_target (eq. 18). Initial allocation universe: NVDA, AAPL, MSFT, SPY, HYG (five assets, satisfying the assignment's rules). The re-optimization of §9.1 ranks the top 100 equities by mean P̂(up), Sharpe, Adjusted Sharpe (Pezier–White), and Sortino, and re-solves the γ-sweep, yielding the MCK/AVGO/PWR Long-Safe portfolio (Sharpe 2.01 at γ=3, AdjSharpe 2.24, Sortino 5.35; Table 15) and a Growth portfolio of LLY 40.4% / AMD 59.6% (μ_p 45.36%, σ_p 29.28%, Sharpe 1.74, Sortino 3.79; Table 20). With constraints removed and the 100-asset universe, Sharpe rises monotonically with γ from 1.87 (γ=2) to 2.52 (γ=6) (Table 18). The short extension relaxes non-negativity for COIN only: w_COIN ∈ [−0.20, 0], proceeds recycled into LLY and AMD, giving γ=3 weights LLY 0.567 / AMD 0.633 / COIN −0.200, μ_p 57.24%, σ_p 35.91%, Sharpe 1.62, gross exposure 1.40 (Tables 22–23) — Sharpe falls because σ rises faster than excess return.

### EVT/GPD leverage overlay (§10.3, Appendix B)

For the leveraged sleeve, daily log returns are volatility-normalized z_t = r_t/σ_daily and exceedances X_t = −(z_t − u_z) for z_t < −u_z (u_z = 1.75 in the parameter tables; −2.5 is given as an example threshold in the text) are fit to a GPD with survival function P(X > x) = p_u (1 + ξ(x − u_z)/β)^{−1/ξ}; one-day ruin probability at leverage L is p_ruin(L) = P(r_t < −1/L). Fitted parameters (Table 24): COIN σ_daily 0.0543, ξ 0.317, β 0.464, p_u 0.061, n=1154; AMD σ 0.0325, ξ −0.046, β 0.603, p_u 0.081, n=1255; LLY σ 0.0201, ξ 0.560, β 0.411, p_u 0.066, n=1255. One-day ruin at L=10: COIN ≈ 5.1%, AMD ≈ 0.8%, LLY ≈ 0.33% (text: 0.8% and 0.24%; Table 25 gives 8.02e−3 and 3.27e−3). The structure: $300,000 equity, short COIN 1×/2×/3× ($270k/$600k/$900k notional ≈ 750/1,667/2,500 shares near $360), COIN falls 4.50833% to $343.77, raising equity to $312,172 / $327,052 / $340,575, then 10% margin supports TRS notionals N_TRS = 10 × E_usable split evenly between AMD and LLY; post-entry short leverage falls to 0.96×/1.84×/2.64× with ruin probabilities 3.4e−9 / 7.1e−5 / 1.2e−3 (one event per ~300M / ~14,000 / ~800 trading days).

## Test setup

10 years of daily prices resampled to month-end; returns/risk statistics over trailing 12/24/60-month windows; fundamentals as TTM sums of the last four quarterly reports (§2.1). Out-of-sample period: 48 rolling monthly windows, Nov-2020 → Oct-2025 (§7); the paper reports no separate post-publication or preserved hold-out period. Universe: 516 tickers from sp500_constituents.csv and nasdaq100_constituents.csv; accrual coverage >95.9% with mean absolute deviation between the two accrual definitions below 0.01 (§2.1). Data sources named: Yahoo Finance (prices, quarterly financials, shares outstanding), Dolthub (accruals_recent.csv, ttm_eps.csv). Survivorship and delisting handling are not addressed. No transaction costs, financing, or borrow costs are modeled; all figures are gross. Benchmark: SPY for beta and downside metrics; the model comparison benchmark is the uncalibrated logistic baseline. µ and Σ for allocation come from 5-year monthly returns with a constant annual risk-free rate of 3% assumed only in the excerpted 12-ETF frontier example (§10, Table 26: unconstrained tangency excess Sharpe 1.057, long-only 0.612).

## Robustness

Tests reported: horizon sensitivity for calibrated logistic (Table 7) — AUC 0.6197 at 12m, 0.607–0.612 at 24m, 0.600–0.605 at 36m with long hit falling 73.5% → 69–71% — described in the text as "modest degradation at longer horizons"; calibration quality (Platt vs. Isotonic reliability curves, correcting raw p≈0.9 buckets that realize ≈0.6); accruals dual-source validation (§2.1); sector/size neutralization of the score (Appendix A); frontier instability documented via subperiod re-estimation on a 12-ETF universe (SPY, QQQ, IWM, EFA, EEM, TLT, IEF, LQD, HYG, GLD, DBC, VNQ; Jan 3, 2006 – Aug 14, 2025) excerpted from the author's companion paper [13]; and the EVT ruin analysis across three names (§10.3). No subperiod decay analysis of the ML signal is reported beyond noting AUC dips "aligned with broad macro inflections."

## Specification search

Reported search: 3 label horizons × 4 model variants (logistic baseline, logistic K=5+calibration, MLP, LightGBM) over 48 OOS windows; γ-sweep over {2, 3, 4, 6}; allocation universe expanded from 20 to 100 equities (§9.1); two accruals definitions compared across 516 tickers; three portfolio architectures (long-safe, growth, short). Only selected results are reported — e.g., the single-snapshot AUC comparison (Table 2) and one representative horizon set (Table 7) — and the top-20 winner table (Table 14) shows 144 observations despite only 48 OOS months, an inconsistency the paper does not explain.

## Limitations and future work

The authors' own: the paper is a class assignment constrained by its reporting schedule (behavioural strategies like PEAD were abandoned for that reason); options were disallowed, so leverage is explored via swaps only; stochastic processes were deliberately avoided "to remain aligned with assignment guidelines." Future work they name: embedding the fundamental metrics into stochastic price models with non-zero quadratic variation, their influence on implied volatility surfaces, interactions with volume and liquidity, and option-pricing/utility-hedging frameworks; they also conjecture the fundamental signals indirectly encode behavioural biases through analyst heuristics and PEAD (§11).

## Separable variants for our pipeline

- Composite Z-score screen (eq. 6), including the neutralized reweighted variant Score = 0.40·Value + 0.40·Quality + 0.20·Stability with size/sector adjustments (Appendix A).
- Probabilistic rank, logistic K=5 + Platt, 12-month horizon label (Table 6).
- Probabilistic rank, two-layer MLP — the paper's best short-selector variant (Table 6).
- Probabilistic rank, LightGBM (Table 6).
- ML-tilted expected return μ̃_i = μ_hist + α_1(P̂_i − 0.5) + α_2·Score_i inside a γ-sweep allocator (eq. 17).
- Bounded-short allocation, w_COIN ∈ [−0.20, 0], with proceeds recycled into longs (Tables 22–23).
- EVT/GPD volatility-normalized ruin-probability sizing for leveraged positions (Appendix B; Tables 24–25).
